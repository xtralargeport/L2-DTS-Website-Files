Where to Play

From grassroots community parks to world-class ovals, Aotearoa has a thriving cricket scene waiting for you. Whether you are looking for a competitive premier league, a social weekend bash with mates, or an introductory program for the kids, there is a club right around the corner.

Find Your Local Association

Cricket in New Zealand is organized across six Major Associations. Pick your region below to find local clubs, tournament draws, and registrations in your backyard:

  • Northern Districts: Covering Northland, Counties Manukau, Waikato, Bay of Plenty, and Poverty Bay.
  • Auckland Cricket: Serving the greater urban Auckland region across 16 massive community clubs.
  • Central Districts: Spanning the lower North Island (Hawke's Bay, Taranaki, Manawatū) and upper South Island (Nelson, Marlborough).
  • Cricket Wellington: Serving the capital city and the surrounding Wellington region.
  • Canterbury Cricket: From Christchurch metro down to South Canterbury, Mid Canterbury, and the West Coast.
  • Otago Cricket: Managing the game across Otago, Southland, and North Otago.

Programs for All Ages & Stages

🏏 Smash Play (Junior Cricket)

Designed for the youngest cricket stars, Smash Play focuses on fast-paced, high-energy games that teach basic skills like hitting, catching, and throwing in a fun environment. Perfect for kids just stepping onto the grass.

🎒 Youth & School Cricket

New Zealand schools and clubs offer everything from mid-week T20 school leagues to highly competitive Saturday morning youth championships.

🏆 Senior Competitive & Social

Ready for the real deal? Join a club to play traditional two-day matches, standard one-dayers, or fast T20 leagues. If you are short on time, look out for local mid-week social corporate leagues or indoor cricket centers near you.

How to Get Started

Ready to lace up your spikes? Follow these simple steps:

Find a Club: Head to the official New Zealand Cricket (NZC) Community Hub or use the PlayHQ platform to search for teams by postcode.

Register Online: Club registrations generally open ahead of the summer season.

Grab the Gear: Most clubs provide heavy team gear (pads, bats, helmets) for junior levels, so you only need the basics to start!
